# Notes

- removed all non-core-feature focused modules

## Additions
- introduce a new *reporting service* and *reporting db* for logging and stats (called async)

## Sync / async calls
- replaces synchronous calls with async calls where possible to avoid tight coupling and achieve higher modularity
    - namely: The *Enrollment Service* will no longer call *Student Service* synchronously to update the student's credit count/progress. It will just publish an EnrollmentCreated event, and the Student Service will consume it asynchronously to perform the update.
    - async calls to *reporting service*

- sync dependencies are still necessary in the *Enrollment Service* for calls to:
    - *Student Service*: to check prerequisites and current credit limits before enrollment is committed.
    - *Course Service*: To check real-time capacity and ticket availability before enrollment is committed.

## Enrollment service
- decoupling the serving from the rest as much as possible
- duplication of data as each container will have its own databases
    - this is needed for horizontal scaling during high demand
    - these will sync in intervals with the "golden-source-of-truth" databases
